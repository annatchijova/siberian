## Cross-cutting log retention evidence

Microsoft's [Event Log Policies specification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/windows_protocols/ms-gpsb/0b9673a7-ce0a-49b4-912b-591efdb37cdf) documents maximum log size and retention modes, including overwrite-as-needed, retention-days, and manual-clear behavior. This establishes that retention configuration affects what can remain; it does not establish a particular host's policy or prove that an acquired log covers a case interval. Those remain case evidence requirements.

## Epistemic status and update rule

- **Documented:** only claims in the cited source pages, within their stated page/product scope.
- **Not tested:** edition/build/UBR/channel cross-product, configuration-specific generation, rollover/wrap survival, and collection/parser behavior.
- **Not inferred:** attacker intent, deliberate deletion, or probability from absence.

Change a row's scope only when a primary source explicitly supports the added product/configuration or a reproducible lab record supports the empirical claim. Record source section, retrieval date, exact configuration, raw evidence hashes, and reviewer. A lab result applies only to its tested image/configuration until independent runs support broader scope.
