## Adaptation note

VIGÍA's current detector is a research starting point, not a validated standalone product. Its artifact status is stored in shared present/absent sets, so status is not tied to a specific action. An unregistered artifact behaves like a non-absence in selectivity denominators even though its status is unknown. Its `fabrication_likelihood` is an uncalibrated weighted score, not an empirical probability.

The SIBERIAN port keeps the concept but has replaced the seed weights with a smaller, source-linked conditional Windows catalog. Unsupported seed entries are deferred pending applicability research; no score or probability is produced.
