## Adaptation note

VIGÍA's current detector is a useful research starting point, not a validated standalone product. In its current form, artifact status is stored in shared present/absent sets, while an unregistered artifact contributes to absence denominators as though its status were known. Its `fabrication_likelihood` also risks sounding like a calibrated probability. SIBERIAN's first implementation must model `unknown` and collection/applicability limits explicitly and avoid interpreting a heuristic score as probability or proof of intent.
